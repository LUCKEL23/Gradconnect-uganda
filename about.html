Purpose and background

Why Grad Connect Uganda?

Graduates can face difficulties moving from education into work. Grad Connect Uganda aims to make freelance work, internships and short projects easier to explore across different fields.

The platform brings together opportunity information, graduate profiles, employer vacancy details and career guidance.

Published evidence

Understanding the challenge

Uganda's 2024 census recorded national unemployment of 12.3% among people aged 15 and above. The census also reported that 42.6% of people aged 15–24 were not in employment, education or training. Neither is a graduate unemployment rate.

The ILO reported 218 young people completing digital-work training in a 2025 pilot; 70 programme graduates had secured 148 paid gigs.
